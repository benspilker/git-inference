The repair bench

From a noisy wheel to a worn-out deck, describe the issue and we’ll help you figure out the next repair step.

01

Wheels & bearings

Wheel and bearing inspection, cleaning, replacement, and setup checks for rough, slow, or noisy roll. We can also check axle nuts and wheel clearance.

02

Trucks & hardware

Truck tightening and adjustment, hardware checks, and replacement of worn mounting bolts or bushings. We’ll look for looseness, uneven turning, and unusual wear.

03

Grip & deck

Grip tape replacement, deck setup, and a visual check for chips, cracks, or delamination. If a deck looks unsafe to ride, we’ll explain what we can see.

04

Setup check

A practical once-over of the complete board: wheels, bearings, trucks, hardware, grip, and deck condition. Useful when something feels wrong and you’re not sure why.

Tell us what you’ve noticed—rattling hardware, wheels that don’t spin freely, worn grip, or a deck that’s taken a hit. We’ll use the details to understand what may need attention.

For damage that could affect safe riding, stop riding the board until it has been checked. A photo can help explain what you’re seeing.
