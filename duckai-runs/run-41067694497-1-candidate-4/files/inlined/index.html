Fogline Skate Repair is an independent skateboard repair shop concept based in San Francisco, California.

Every board tells you something. A rattling bolt, uneven wheel wear, or grip that’s lost its bite can point to a simple fix—or a part that needs a closer look. We keep the conversation practical and the focus on getting your setup ride-ready.
