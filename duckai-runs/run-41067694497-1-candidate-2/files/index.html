Ask about your setup
01

Truck tune-up

Check for loose or uneven hardware, adjust truck tightness to your preference, and look over bushings for visible wear or damage.

02

Wheels & bearings

Inspect wheel wear and bearing condition, clear away grime, and check that each wheel spins freely. Ask about noisy, rough, or stuck wheels.

03

Grip tape refresh

Replace worn grip tape for a fresh-feeling deck surface. Mention any old adhesive or deck damage so it can be considered before work begins.

04

Hardware & setup check

Look over bolts, nuts, trucks, and deck for looseness or visible wear. Bring the whole board and explain what you notice while riding.
