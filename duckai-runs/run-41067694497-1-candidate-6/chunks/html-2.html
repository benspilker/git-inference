The Fogline approach

Fix what
makes sense.

Good board care starts with a look at the whole setup. Describe the issue, share what you’ve noticed, and ask what might be worth checking before deciding on a repair.

  • Start with the problem you’re feeling on the board.
  • Check the affected parts and how they fit together.
  • Talk through repair options and parts you may need.
  • If the deck is damaged, consider whether replacement is safer.
