Loose hardware, rough bearings, a worn grip job—small issues can change the whole ride. Fogline focuses on practical fixes and clear conversations about what your board needs.

Bring the setup, describe what feels off, and ask about the repair. We’ll help you figure out a sensible next step—without making promises before seeing the board.

Repair bench

What we
can look at.

Tell us what’s happening with your setup. These are common repair and maintenance requests to ask about.

01

Hardware & setup check

Ask about loose or mismatched mounting hardware, truck adjustments, and a basic check of how your components fit together.

02

Grip tape refresh

For peeling, worn, or tired grip: discuss removing old tape, preparing the deck surface, and applying a fresh grip layer.

03

Wheels & bearings

Ask about wheel swaps, bearing cleaning, or replacing bearings that feel rough. Bring your parts if you already have them.

04

Deck condition

We can discuss visible chips, worn edges, and deck condition. Some damage may call for replacement rather than repair.

The Fogline approach

Fix what
makes sense.

Good board care starts with a look at the whole setup. Describe the issue, share what you’ve noticed, and ask what might be worth checking before deciding on a repair.

  • Start with the problem you’re feeling on the board.
  • Check the affected parts and how they fit together.
  • Talk through repair options and parts you may need.
  • If the deck is damaged, consider whether replacement is safer.
