Small fixes. Better rides.

Every board wears differently. These are common repair and setup needs—not a one-size-fits-all checklist. The right fix depends on the condition of your parts and how you ride.

01

Wheel & bearing care

Check wheel wear and bearing fit, clear out grit, and replace worn or damaged bearings when needed. We can also help track down a wheel that’s rubbing or not spinning freely.

02

Truck tune-ups

Inspect kingpin and axle hardware, adjust truck tightness, and check for loose or damaged bushings. A careful tune-up can help your board turn more predictably.

03

Grip & hardware

Replace worn grip tape, check mounting hardware, and snug up a rattling setup. If a bolt, nut, or other part is damaged, we’ll flag it before it becomes a bigger problem.

04

Deck & setup check

Look over a deck for cracks, delamination, or other damage, then check how the full setup fits together. We’ll explain what looks serviceable and what may need replacing.
