The Fogline approach

Good rides start with a setup you trust.

Fogline Skate Repair is a fictional independent shop concept based in San Francisco. The focus is straightforward: inspect the issue, explain practical repair options, and help you understand what your board needs before work begins.

What we can look at

Small fixes.
Better feel.

Describe what’s happening with your board. We’ll help you figure out whether it needs a tune-up, a part replacement, or a closer inspection.

01

Wheels & bearings

Wheel swaps, bearing cleaning or replacement, and checks for drag, grit, or uneven wear that can affect how your board rolls.

02

Trucks & hardware

Truck adjustments, kingpin and bushing checks, and help with loose, stripped, or mismatched mounting hardware.

03

Grip & setup

Grip tape replacement and setup checks, including a look at deck condition, hardware fit, and how your parts work together.

04

Deck assessment

Inspection for cracks, delamination, and worn edges. We’ll explain what appears repairable and when a replacement deck may make more sense.

A simple place to start

Bring the board. Bring the story.

A clear description helps us understand the problem. Tell us what feels different, which part is affected, and whether you’ve noticed any damage. If you can, include a photo of the area you’re concerned about.
