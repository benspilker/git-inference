The Fogline approach

Know what’s under your feet.

A repair starts with the board and the problem you’re noticing. We’ll help narrow down what might be causing it, explain the options in plain language, and keep the focus on parts that need attention.

Not sure what’s wrong? A brief description and a photo of the setup can be a useful place to start.

Before you roll

A quick board check.

Look for loose truck bolts, cracked or chipped deck edges, wheels that wobble, and bearings that feel gritty or seize up. Check that the hardware sits securely and that each wheel turns without rubbing the deck.

If anything looks cracked, bent, or unstable, stop riding until it’s checked. Share what you noticed when you ask about a repair; it helps make the conversation more specific.

Start with a question

What’s happening with your board?

Describe the issue, mention any parts you already suspect, or include a photo of the setup. We’ll use those details to start a repair conversation.
