A loose truck, rattling hardware, or worn-out wheel can change the whole feel of a setup. Fogline is a fictional San Francisco skate-repair shop concept focused on careful checks and clear repair options—so you can decide what makes sense for your board.

Repair menu

The parts that keep you rolling.

Start with what feels off. A careful look at the full setup helps narrow down what needs attention—and what doesn’t.

  • 01

    Hardware & trucks

    Check for loose mounting hardware, uneven truck response, and worn bushings. Tightening and adjustment can help; damaged parts may need replacement.

  • 02

    Wheels & bearings

    Inspect wheels for uneven wear and bearings for grit or rough rotation. Cleaning, wheel rotation, or replacement may be appropriate depending on their condition.

  • 03

    Deck & grip

    Check the deck for cracks, chips, and delamination, and look over grip tape for lifting edges. We’ll explain what can be tidied up and when a deck may be past repair.

  • 04

    Setup check

    A practical once-over of the complete board: hardware tightness, wheel movement, truck feel, and visible wear. Useful when something feels wrong but you’re not sure where to start.

No mystery fixes

Know what’s going on with your setup.

Tell us what you’ve noticed—rattle, wobble, rough roll, or visible damage. The next step is a clear explanation of what to inspect and what repair options might fit. No guessed-at prices or promises before the board is assessed.
