A little care goes a long way

Good boards
deserve another lap.

City miles leave their marks: a chipped edge, a wheel that won’t spin freely, grip worn smooth under your front foot. A small repair can make a familiar board feel ready for the next session.

Fogline is a fictional San Francisco skate-repair shop concept built around practical, straightforward board care. Tell us what’s going on with your setup and we’ll help you figure out the next step.

What we can look at

Small fixes.
More good sessions.

Not sure what your board needs? Describe the issue when you reach out. We’ll help you narrow it down.

01

Grip tape refresh

Remove worn or peeling grip, clean the deck surface, and apply fresh tape. Mention any preferred grip style or cutout when you ask.

02

Wheels & bearings

Check wheel wear and bearing spin, clean accessible dirt, and inspect for roughness or wobble. We can help identify what may need replacing.

03

Truck tune-up

Inspect truck hardware and bushings, check for looseness, and adjust the setup to help address rattles, uneven turning, or a wobbly ride.

04

Deck & hardware check

Take a closer look at mounting hardware, stress cracks, chips, and delamination. A damaged deck may need replacing rather than repair.
