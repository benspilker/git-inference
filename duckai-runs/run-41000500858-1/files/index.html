Repair menu

Small fixes.
More sessions.

Tell us what’s going on with your board. We’ll help identify the issue and discuss a sensible repair.

01

Deck & grip

Grip tape replacement, hardware checks, and a look at chips, cracks, or delamination. A damaged deck may need replacing rather than repairing.

02

Trucks & hardware

Check for loose or mismatched hardware, worn bushings, and truck issues. We can help with tightening, part swaps, and basic setup adjustments.

03

Wheels & bearings

Inspect wheel wear and bearing condition, clear out dirt where possible, and assess whether cleaning, adjustment, or replacement parts make sense.

A considered repair

Know what’s going on under your feet.

We start by looking over the whole setup, not just the noisy part. You’ll get a plain-language explanation of what we find and which adjustments or replacement parts could help.
