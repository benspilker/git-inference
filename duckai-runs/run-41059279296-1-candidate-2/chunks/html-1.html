Bench work, not guesswork

Repair the ride.

From a noisy wheel to a deck that has seen one session too many, here are the kinds of fixes this shop concept is built around.

01

Hardware & setup

Check loose or mismatched mounting hardware, adjust truck tightness, and get your setup feeling more predictable underfoot.

02

Wheels & bearings

Inspect wheel wear, clean accessible bearing grime, and replace worn parts when a wheel is rough, rattling, or refusing to roll smoothly.

03

Grip & deck care

Refresh worn grip tape and assess chips, cracks, or delamination. A damaged deck may need replacing rather than patching.
