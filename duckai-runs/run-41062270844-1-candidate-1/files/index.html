The Fogline approach

Small fixes.
Better sessions.

Loose hardware, rough bearings, worn grip, or a truck that will not sit right can change the feel of a whole ride. We focus on practical board care: identify the issue, explain the options, and help you get your setup rolling again.

Not sure what your board needs? Send an inquiry with a short description and we can start there.

Repair menu

Back to
good grip.

Tell us what feels off and we can help narrow down the fix. Work depends on the board’s condition and the parts it needs.

01

Hardware & setup check

We check truck and deck hardware for looseness, worn or mismatched parts, and fit. A setup check can include tightening and basic adjustments where appropriate.

02

Wheels & bearings

For rough-spinning wheels or a rattly ride, we inspect the wheels and bearings, clean serviceable parts, and discuss replacement when cleaning will not do the job.

03

Grip tape refresh

Old, peeling, or damaged grip can be removed and replaced. We’ll check the deck surface first and talk through whether a fresh sheet is a sensible fit.

04

Deck & truck assessment

Cracks, chips, wheel bite, or steering that feels uneven deserve a closer look. We’ll explain what we find and whether adjustment, a part swap, or a new deck makes sense.

What to tell us

A few useful details help us understand the problem and suggest a sensible inspection.

  • What feels different when you ride?
  • Which part of the board seems affected?
  • Has anything been changed or replaced recently?
  • Include a photo if it helps show the issue.
