The repair bench

Get your setup back.

From a quick hardware check to a full rebuild, start with what feels off. We’ll help narrow down the repair and what your board needs.

01

Deck & grip

Grip tape replacement, hardware swaps, and deck inspections for chips, cracks, or mounting-hole wear. A damaged deck may need replacing rather than repairing.

02

Trucks & setup

Truck tightening and alignment, bushing checks, and help tracking down wheel bite, uneven turning, or a setup that doesn’t feel right underfoot.

03

Wheels & bearings

Wheel and bearing inspection, cleaning, replacement, and rotation. We’ll check for flat spots, rough spin, and hardware that needs attention.

04

Full board check

A practical once-over of deck, trucks, wheels, bearings, and hardware, with clear next steps for safe riding and routine upkeep.

A closer look

Good repairs start with a good check.

A wheel that won’t spin freely can point to dirt, a worn bearing, or hardware that’s too tight. A board that turns unevenly might need a truck adjustment—or a closer look at its bushings.
